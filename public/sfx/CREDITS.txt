  (voice dataset: CC0, https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets; piper-voices repo: MIT). Slovak text is

  EQ, 4:1 compressor, short room, loudnorm -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP), 44.1 kHz mono MP3 128 kbps.
  Scripts: scripts/koleso-vo/make_vo_v2.sh (gen_piper_v2.py, process_v2.sh), lines.tsv.
  CAVEAT (commercial use): the model card says the voice is "Finetuned from U.S. English lessac voice";
  the lessac dataset licence (https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html)
  is a "Research Licence Agreement" excluding commercial use. Whether that carries over to a fine-tuned
  model is not legally clear (the same applies to the earlier v1 voice sk_SK-lili-medium). Before a
  commercial release: get a legal opinion, or replace with a licensed paid TTS / voice actor recording.
